Proto: Ship The Prototype

Proto is hypothetical brand created by me and my friend as a part of course curriculum.
The project was focused on creating a space for designers and design students to showcase their works and sell their prototypes. The platform aimed to foster a community of creativity and innovation, allowing users to share their design concepts and connect with potential buyers.
